Our Story

Experience woven into every garment.

For more than 25 years, the people behind Chappelle Dry Cleaners have worked in dry cleaning, laundry and ironing — learning, order by order, what it takes to keep a garment looking the way it should.

Since 2018, that experience has been at work here in Edmonton, where every piece that comes through our doors is treated with the same care, whether it's a weekday shirt or a wedding gown.

It's a simple idea, carried through every order: your clothes are in experienced hands.
